# Retrospective: S50.1 Schema Updates (Foundation)

## What went well
- The scope was clearly defined and executed quickly.
- We caught the entity name error early (`Sys_Roles` vs `Rol`).
- The addition of `contexto_id` to `Sys_Graph_Edges` establishes the foundation for Atomic Draft Versioning safely.

## What could be improved
- We could have checked `Schema_Engine.js` before writing the scope to prevent the `Sys_Roles` confusion.

## Learnings
- Agile roles are stored in `Rol`, while system security roles are stored in `Sys_Roles`. This distinction is important for ABAC vs Business logic.
