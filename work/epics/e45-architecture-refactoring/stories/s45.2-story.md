# S45.2: Leader Auto-Provisioning en Cascada

## User Story

**As a** user registering employees or importing them in bulk
**I want** the system to automatically provision their direct leaders if they don't exist
**So that** the topological lineage is correctly maintained without forcing me to manually create every single manager in the chain beforehand.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **MUST**: The `AutoProvisionLiderDirecto` interceptor should check if the provided `lider_directo` exists in the database.
- **MUST**: If the leader does not exist, the interceptor must create a stub record with `workspace_sync_status: 'pending'` in O(1) time without blocking the UI with recursive lookups.
- **MUST**: The `Job_WorkspaceSync` background job must pick up these stub records, hydrate them from Workspace, and recursively delegate the provisioning of *their* leaders by applying the interceptor again.
- **MUST**: The `Job_WorkspaceSync` must correctly emit `Sys_Graph_Edges` payload for `PERSONA_LIDER_DIRECTO` relationships to maintain SCD-2 integrity.

## Context

En E44 implementamos auto-provisionamiento de cargos. Sin embargo, al registrar una persona, su `lider_directo` también podría no existir en la base de datos local. Intentar consultar toda la cadena jerárquica de líderes sincrónicamente contra Google Workspace bloquearía la UI o provocaría timeouts en cargas masivas. 
Por lo tanto, adoptamos una estrategia de **Consistencia Eventual en Cascada**: se crea un registro temporal instantáneo para el jefe directo, delegando al Job asíncrono la tarea de rellenar sus datos reales y descubrir al *jefe del jefe* en iteraciones subsecuentes.
