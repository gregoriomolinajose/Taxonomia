# Architecture Review (Story Scope)
**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Target:** Story S49.12 (Architecture Stabilization)

## Context
Story S49.12 was introduced to resolve architectural and technical debt identified in the S49.11 iteration:
1. Data Hydration incompleteness for secondary entities (Lookups within Lookups, specifically `subtitleLookup` causing `TXSearchable` errors).
2. Monkey-patching of the Stepper's `goToSection` method by the `FormRenderer_UI` to manage footer progress dots.

## Evaluation (Beck's Four Rules of Simple Design)

### 1. Passes the Tests (Correctness)
**Status:** Pass
- The `FormEngine_Resolvers.client.js` now correctly parses `subtitleLookup` fields and appends them to the hydration queue as `_virtual_subtitle_` relations. This natively loads the required entities (e.g., `Cargo`) into the `window.DataStore` without requiring asynchronous data fetching in `TXSearchable`, fulfilling the core AC-1 requirement.
- The `UI_FormStepper.client.js` fires `onStepChange(currentIndex, totalSteps)` internally at the end of `goToSection`.
- The `FormRenderer_UI.client.js` successfully receives this callback and updates the DOM, successfully removing the monkey-patch and avoiding side-effects.

### 2. Reveals Intention (Clarity)
**Status:** Pass
- The addition of `onStepChange` clearly communicates the intention: the Stepper manages the flow and broadcasts its state changes, while the Renderer listens to update its own specific UI components. This is a much clearer contract than redefining prototype methods on the fly.
- The virtual `targetEntity` hydration in the engine clearly labels itself with `_virtual_subtitle_`, making its purpose transparent in the parallel fetching array.

### 3. No Duplication (DRY)
**Status:** Pass
- The hydration logic is cleanly integrated into the existing mapping loop.
- The UI updating logic was not duplicated; it was merely moved out of an override and into a clean callback.

### 4. Fewest Elements (Simplicity)
**Status:** Pass
- No new classes, files, or complex event busses were introduced. The callback approach is the simplest valid pattern for a 1-to-1 relationship between the parent Renderer and the child Stepper.

## Architectural Health Assessment

### Abstraction Leaks
- **Resolved**: The Stepper's internal mechanics are no longer hijacked by the Renderer. The abstraction leak identified in S49.11 is completely mitigated.

### Data Layer Separation
- **Improved**: `TXSearchable` remains a pure, synchronous UI component. The data-loading responsibility has been cleanly shifted back to `FormEngine_Resolvers`, maintaining the architectural purity of the DataStore pattern.

## Recommendations
- **Approve**. The changes implemented precisely address the debt while keeping the footprint extremely small and localized. No further refactoring is required for these specific components.
