# Epic Brief: Organizational Chart Visualization

## Hypothesis
If we implement an interactive ApexTree chart for employee relationships, users will be able to easily visualize the organizational hierarchy, identify roles, and distinguish departments by color, leading to better structural comprehension.

## Success Metrics
- A functional, interactive ApexTree chart is rendered.
- Hierarchy correctly maps relationships between employees.
- Node information displays Name, Surname, Role (Cargo), and Department (by color).

## Appetite
1-2 weeks.

## Rabbit Holes
- Handling very large trees rendering performance.
- Complex styling within the SVG of ApexCharts.
- Re-calculating the graph repeatedly instead of caching.
